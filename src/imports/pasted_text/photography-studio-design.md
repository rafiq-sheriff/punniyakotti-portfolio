Design a premium, modern, visually immersive website for a professional Photography & Videography Studio that specializes in capturing weddings, corporate events, business events, private celebrations, concerts, cultural events, and all types of special occasions.

The website's PRIMARY FOCUS is photography and videography. The images and videos must do most of the storytelling. Create a website where visitors immediately understand the quality, creativity, cinematic style, and professionalism of the studio through the visual design.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Premium, cinematic, editorial, modern, artistic, and sophisticated
- Clean and minimal interface with strong visual hierarchy
- Image-first design
- Large, edge-to-edge photography
- Cinematic video sections
- Elegant typography
- Generous whitespace
- Subtle animations and transitions
- Avoid generic photography-template designs
- Make the website feel like a high-end creative production studio
- The design should feel unique and memorable
- Use a sophisticated neutral color palette such as warm white, charcoal, black, soft gray, and subtle beige accents
- Avoid excessive colors
- Use typography with a premium editorial feel
- Strong contrast between typography and photography
- Rounded corners should be used sparingly
- Avoid excessive cards, gradients, icons, and unnecessary UI elements

CREATE THE FOLLOWING WEBSITE STRUCTURE:

1. NAVIGATION
Create a minimal premium navigation bar:
- Studio logo / wordmark
- Home
- Portfolio
- Services
- About
- Stories / Journal
- Contact
- Primary CTA: "Book a Session"

Keep navigation clean and unobtrusive. On the homepage, navigation can overlay the hero image.

2. HERO SECTION
Create a full-screen cinematic hero section.

Use a stunning full-screen wedding/event photograph or cinematic event video as the background.

Headline:
"WE CAPTURE MOMENTS THAT LAST FOREVER."

Supporting text:
"Photography, films, and aerial storytelling for weddings, events, brands, and unforgettable moments."

CTA buttons:
"Explore Our Work"
"Book Your Date"

Include a subtle scroll indicator.

The hero should immediately communicate that this is a premium photography and videography studio.

The hero image should be the strongest visual element on the entire page.

3. INTRODUCTION / STUDIO STATEMENT
Create a minimal editorial section introducing the studio.

Example headline:
"YOUR MOMENTS. OUR PERSPECTIVE."

Short paragraph explaining that the studio captures authentic emotions, atmosphere, details, people, and stories through photography, cinematic films, and aerial cinematography.

Use an asymmetrical layout with one large photograph and smaller supporting image.

4. SERVICES SECTION
Create a visually rich services section rather than a traditional card grid.

Services:
- Wedding Photography
- Wedding Films
- Corporate & Business Events
- Event Photography
- Event Videography
- Drone & Aerial Cinematography
- Pre-Wedding Photography
- Brand & Commercial Photography
- Social Media Content

Each service should have a large supporting image.

When hovering over a service, reveal a subtle title, description, and "View Work" interaction.

5. FEATURED WORK / PORTFOLIO
This should be one of the most visually impressive sections.

Create an editorial masonry-style portfolio/gallery.

Categories:
ALL
WEDDINGS
EVENTS
CORPORATE
BRANDS
DRONE

Use large high-quality images with different aspect ratios.

Do NOT make every image the same size.

Create an intentional magazine/editorial composition using:
- Full-width images
- Portrait images
- Landscape images
- Smaller supporting images
- Large visual gaps

Each project should feel like a story rather than a simple gallery.

Example projects:
"Arjun & Ananya — Wedding Story"
"Urban Business Summit"
"Coastal Wedding — Chennai"
"Annual Corporate Gala"
"Destination Wedding"
"Luxury Brand Campaign"

6. CINEMATIC VIDEO SECTION
Create a large cinematic video showcase section.

Use a wide 16:9 video thumbnail with a large play button.

Headline:
"EVERY FRAME TELLS A STORY."

Add a short description about cinematic wedding films, event films, brand films, and highlight videos.

Include a secondary link:
"Watch Showreel"

The section should visually feel like a film production website.

CTA:

- Large image ratios
- Full-bleed photography
- High-quality visual hierarchy
- Minimal text
- Image-led storytelling
- Premium typography
- Smooth scrolling feel
- Subtle hover interactions

- Elegant page transitions
- Video play interactions

- Smooth gallery transitions

The website must NOT look like a generic photography template.

Think of the visual quality and art direction of:
- premium editorial magazines
- luxury wedding studios
- cinematic production companies
- high-end creative agencies
- modern portfolio websites

The website should feel like a combination of:
LUXURY + CINEMA + EDITORIAL + EMOTION + STORYTELLING.

The photography should always be the primary visual element.

Do not overcrowd the interface with text, cards, icons, gradients, or decorative elements.

Create a polished, production-ready Figma website design with consistent spacing, typography, responsive behavior, reusable components, and a sophisticated visual system.
